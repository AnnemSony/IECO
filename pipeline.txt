Full Pipeline Overview:
Image Masking:
Extract the roof area from aerial images using polygon masks.
Focus the analysis on roof regions and discard irrelevant areas.
Point Cloud Extraction:
Use spatial alignment techniques (GPS coordinates) to map the 2D roof regions to 3D point cloud data.
Extract only the roof-related points from the 3D point cloud.
3D Reconstruction:
Process the extracted points to remove noise and refine the data.
Use surface reconstruction methods (like triangulation or Poisson reconstruction) to build a 3D model of the roof.
Optionally, apply texture or refine the model to enhance realism and accuracy.
